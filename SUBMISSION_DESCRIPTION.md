## Briefed — Meeting Prep Agent

**Briefed** helps people walk into recurring customer meetings already knowing what was discussed, promised, and preferred last time.

### What the agent does

After a meeting, you log a short debrief (discussions, decisions, commitments, concerns and preferences). Briefed retains that interaction in long-term memory. Before the next meeting with the same contact, it recalls relevant facts and generates a personalized meeting brief — with a clear view of which memories were used.

### Who it is for

Account executives, customer success, founders, and anyone who runs repeat B2B conversations and cannot afford to forget open loops.

### The problem it solves

Relationship context lives in scattered notes or disappears between calls. Generic prep does not surface a pending document promise or a recurring cost concern. Missed follow-ups and cold restarts are common.

### How long-term memory changes its behavior

Without memory, Briefed can only give generic advice (discuss the project, ask about timeline). With accumulated retained interactions, preparation becomes specific: open commitments, recurring concerns, communication preferences, and prior outcomes for that contact. More retained history means richer recall and more useful next-meeting prep.

### Why Hindsight is central

Hindsight is the agent’s long-term memory — retain after interactions, recall before preparation. Application storage holds contacts and meeting metadata; Hindsight holds the relationship intelligence the brief is grounded on. Without it, Briefed would be a one-shot prep chatbot, not a memory-backed meeting agent.
